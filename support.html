Frequently asked questions

How do I create a room or a place?

On the home screen or in the Places tab, tap "Add place". Inside a room, use the plus button to add cupboards, shelves or boxes. Places can be nested as deep as you like and moved later.

How do I add an item?

Tap plus at the bottom. The camera opens, you take a photo of the item, give it a name and choose the place. "Save and add another" is the fastest way to capture many things in the same place.

How do I find something again?

In the Search tab, type the name, a search term or the type, for example "Tools". Every result shows the full path, such as Basement › Shelf › Box 3, and the photo of the place.

What does "Checked out" mean?

An item that is currently not in its place, for example lent out or in use. Mark it in the detail view and note where it is right now. On the home screen you find all checked-out items under "Out and about". Its place stays saved.

How do I move a place?

Open the place, tap the menu at the top right and choose "Move to…". Rooms fit into buildings, cupboards and boxes fit anywhere. Everything inside moves along.

How do reminders work?

In the detail view of an item, pick a date and time. At that time the app reminds you and shows right away where the item is. Reminders are part of Premium.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud. Image recognition runs on the device as well. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I back up my data or switch phones?

In Settings, "Create backup" saves everything into one file, for example to iCloud Drive or via AirDrop to your computer. On the new device choose "Restore backup".

What does the app cost?

The app is free for up to 50 items. Premium removes the limit and adds reminders and, soon, sharing with your family. You can pay monthly, yearly or once. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings under "Delete all data", or by deleting the app. Since nothing is stored on servers, that removes everything.
